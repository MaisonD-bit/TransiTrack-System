# TransiTrack — Combined Usability Survey (All Roles)

**Purpose:** One coherent **10-question** usability instrument for **TransiTrack**, aligned with the structure and tone of **Capstone Sample.pdf** (approximately **pages 106–108** for usability narrative and **Table 20**–style Likert results). The same sample chapter continues with **performance testing** tables (**~pp. 109–113**); use those pages for *separate* timing or load tests, not for these Likert items.

**Who answers:** Participants use the parts of TransiTrack that match their role—**Terminal Manager** (web), **Bus Operator** (web), **Bus Driver** (Transit mobile), or **Commuter** (Commuters mobile). Each person completes **one** questionnaire about **the application(s) they actually used** during the test period.

**Sources merged:** `TerminalManager_Usability_Survey.md`, `BusOperator_Usability_Survey.md`, `TransitApp_Usability_Survey.md` (driver + commuter parts).

## Survey introduction (thesis-ready, after Capstone Sample)

This phase of testing evaluates the **user experience** of the TransiTrack platform (web panels and mobile apps). It gathers perceptions of **stability**, **navigation**, **visual design**, **feature correctness**, **responsiveness**, **role-specific work**, **maps and live data**, **notifications**, **coordination** (e.g. chat), and **overall satisfaction**. The table below summarizes satisfaction ratings from participants who completed the combined survey.

**Likert scale (five points), matching the sample:**

| Code | Rating              | Numeric weight (for average) |
|------|---------------------|------------------------------|
| **SA** | Strongly Agree    | 5 |
| **A**  | Agree             | 4 |
| **N**  | Neutral           | 3 |
| **D**  | Disagree          | 2 |
| **SD** | Strongly Disagree | 1 |

**Average score:** For each question, multiply the count in each category by its weight, sum, and divide by **Total** participants, as in the capstone sample.

## The 10 combined questions (easy to read)

1. **The TransiTrack application(s) I used for my role did not crash or freeze during normal use.**

2. **TransiTrack was easy to navigate, and the screens relevant to my role were easy to understand.**

3. **The design and layout of the interface(s) I used were visually appealing and professional.**

4. **The features I needed for my role (dashboards, schedules, routes, terminal tools, driver tools, or commuter booking—whichever applied) functioned as I expected.**

5. **Loading times for pages, lists, maps, and data felt acceptable for my daily tasks or travel.**

6. **Core tasks for my role—such as managing schedules or routes, terminal spaces or approvals, accepting trips and reporting issues, or finding routes and paying fares—were straightforward to complete.**

7. **Real-time or map-based information (e.g. bus position, route map, live buses, or terminal views) was accurate and useful when my role relied on it.**

8. **Notifications and alerts about schedules, delays, parking, assignments, or service changes were timely and easy to understand.**

9. **Coordination tools I used in TransiTrack (such as in-app chat or messaging with operators, terminal staff, drivers, or related alerts) worked smoothly when I needed them.**

10. **Overall, I am satisfied with TransiTrack for my role and would recommend it to colleagues or other travelers.**
