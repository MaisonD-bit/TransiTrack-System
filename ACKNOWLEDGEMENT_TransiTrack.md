# ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

**TransiTrack**  
*A Comprehensive Web–Mobile Application for Streamlining Public Transportation Operations in Cebu City*

---

With deep appreciation, we recognize everyone who helped bring **TransiTrack** to completion. This capstone grew from many conversations, reviews, and trials—each suggestion, critique, and demonstration nudged the system closer to something we could be proud to present.

We thank our **Project Adviser**, **Engr. Vicente Patalita III**, for his steady guidance, technical insight, and encouragement from early design through implementation, testing, and final evaluation. His questions often revealed gaps we had overlooked and pushed us toward clearer architecture and more honest reporting of what the software could—and could not—do in the field.

We are equally grateful to our **Project Coordinator**, **Sir. Temothy Homecillo**, for clarifying requirements, aligning our work with academic milestones, and helping us stay focused when several parts of the platform—web panels, APIs, and mobile clients—competed for the same deadlines.

We are grateful to our **panel**—**Ms. Khiara Danielle Rubia** (Panel Chairman) and **Mr. Roderick A. Bandalan** (Panelist)—for their careful reading of our manuscript, probing questions during review, and practical recommendations that strengthened our documentation, testing narrative, and defense preparation.

We also thank peers, laboratory staff, and instructors who shared knowledge of **Laravel**, **Ionic / Angular**, **databases**, **maps**, and **integrations** (payments, messaging, notifications). Their patience with repeated debugging sessions helped us translate the **multi-role** vision in our **TransiTrack Must Haves** specification—spanning **commuters**, **drivers**, **terminal managers**, and **bus operators**—into working web panels and mobile experiences rather than diagrams alone.

We extend heartfelt thanks to the **terminal managers** and **bus drivers** who gave us their **time**, **attention**, and **honest feedback**. Whether in walkthroughs, pilot use of the apps, or informal conversations about schedules, bays, safety, and the pace of real operations, they paused busy shifts to test flows and comment on clarity, speed, and usefulness. Those moments shaped improvements we could not have guessed from lectures or laboratory exercises alone.

We appreciate others who offered **practical insight** on public transport in **Cebu City**, terminal procedures, dispatch habits, and operator priorities. Their perspectives helped us align schedules, capacity awareness, incident reporting, and coordination tools with **real operational needs** instead of idealized scenarios on a whiteboard.

We thank **our institution** and the **School of Computer Studies** for laboratories, internet access, software tools, and a learning culture that balances theory with hands-on building—resources without which designing, integrating, and demonstrating **TransiTrack** would have been far more difficult.

Finally, this work is both an **academic milestone** and a meaningful step toward **clearer, safer, and more manageable public transportation** through integrated web and mobile systems. For the opportunity to pursue that goal—and for every person who helped us move it closer to reality—we are truly grateful.
