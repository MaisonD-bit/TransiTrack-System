## Summary of Bus Operator Usability Testing

This survey evaluates the following key areas:
- **Stability & Performance**: Application reliability, loading times, system responsiveness
- **Operational Features**: Route management, scheduling, tracking, notifications
- **Communication**: Driver messaging and alert systems
- **Data Management**: Reporting, analytics, passenger information
- **User Experience**: Navigation, design, ease of use
- **Security & Trust**: Data protection, feature reliability
- **Overall Satisfaction**: General satisfaction and recommendation likelihood
